National Capital

Islamabad

The federal capital of Pakistan, renowned worldwide for its high quality of life, lush green Margalla hills, and iconic Faisal Mosque architecture.

Iconic Landmarks & Famous Places

Badshahi Mosque

Lahore, Punjab — Iconic Mughal era architecture built in red sandstone.

Faisal Mosque

Islamabad — The largest mosque in Pakistan located at the foothills of Margalla.

Minar-e-Pakistan

Lahore, Punjab — Historic national monument representing the Pakistan Resolution.

Arabian Sea (Clifton / Gwadar)

Karachi & Gwadar Coastlines — Deep blue waters along southern trade routes.
